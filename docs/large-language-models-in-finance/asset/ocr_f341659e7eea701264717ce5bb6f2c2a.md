# 2

Foundations and System Design of Financial LLMs

This chapter examines the core architectural and engineering foundations that power modern financial AI. We will look at two complementary perspectives: a **theoretical view**, which explains why transformers, attention mechanisms, and optimization schemes work; and an **engineering view**, which focuses on how to build, scale, and deploy these models in financial systems.

Conceptual intuition on these topics was introduced in the previous chapter. Here, we move beyond that to focus on training dynamics and infrastructure decisions that practitioners need when building or selecting LLM-based solutions for financial use cases.

We begin with a technical examination of the **Transformer architecture**, the foundational model behind today's LLMs. We then move to practical implementation, covering the critical art of **prompt engineering**, the architecture of **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** for connecting models with current and auditable knowledge, and the complete pipeline for building a financial LLM from scratch. Because this is a system-design chapter, we also indicate where alignment, hallucination control, evaluation, and governance enter the architecture rather than treating them as afterthoughts.

In a nutshell, the following topics will be covered in this chapter:

+   - Examining the Transformer architecture
+   - Tokenization and embeddings
+   - Pre-training fundamentals
+   - Prompt engineering
+   - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and grounded financial systems
+   - How to build an LLM from scratch
