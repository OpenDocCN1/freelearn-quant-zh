## Contributors

## About the author

**Miquel Noguer i Alonso** is a seasoned finance professional with over 30 years of experience in quantitative finance. He co-founded the *Artificial Intelligence Finance Institute (AIFI)* and has held senior positions at leading financial institutions, including serving as *Executive Director at UBS and Chief Investment Officer at Andbank*. Miquel is also an active academic, teaching AI and fintech courses at NYU, Columbia University, and other top institutions. He holds an MBA and a degree in business administration from ESADE, as well as a PhD in quantitative finance from UNED. Miquel has published extensively on *AI in finance* and is a co-editor of the *Journal of Machine Learning in Finance*.

## About the reviewers

**Hardik Wadhwa** is a Director of Product Development at Mastercard, leading initiatives at the intersection of identity, AI-driven fraud prevention, and optimization. With deep expertise in fintech across payments, data science, and AI, he began his career as a data scientist and engineer and now leads product and business development to scale impact and drive growth. He holds more than 20 patents and has published more than eight research papers in machine learning and AI. He earned his Integrated Master’s in Mathematics and Computing from IIT (BHU), Varanasi. He builds AI-driven products and solutions that drive revenue and real-world impact.

**Daniel Wu** is a technology executive and AI scientist with over 20 years of engineering and leadership experience across finance, tech, and healthcare. Blending deep technical roots with strategic vision, he specializes in driving responsible AI transformation and recently served as Chief AI Scientist for Global Banking at JPMorgan Chase. A champion of technology democratization, he is a course facilitator for Stanford’s AI Professional Program. Daniel is a sought-after international keynote speaker, strategic adviser, and co-author of books on agentic and generative AI. He is dedicated to building innovative, high-performing teams and shaping the future of enterprise intelligence.
