# Automated performance tuning

Automated systems can adjust batch sizes, replica counts, routing, quantization, or cache allocation, but only within approved safety and cost envelopes. A multi-objective tuning problem can be written as

min_{θ ∈ Θ} (L_{99}(θ), -C_{throughput}(θ), C_{TCO}(θ), E_{quality}(θ)), \quad (10.3)

subject to availability, compliance, and minimum-quality constraints. Here θ denotes the serving configuration, L_{99} is p99 latency, and E_{quality} is a task-specific error measure. Tuning should use held-out workloads and canary limits because optimizing against recent production traffic can overfit to a temporary regime.

# 10.5 Illustrative case study: Low-latency trading support

This section is a worked architecture and capacity-planning example. All numerical results are illustrative, not audited production measurements, and should not be interpreted as investment-performance evidence. The workflow supports analysts and downstream signal systems; a generative LLM is not placed directly in the order-execution path.

# 10.5.1 System requirements and constraints

The scenario ingests timestamped news and alternative-text events, enriches them with market context, and produces a low-latency sentiment and relevance score. A separate generative service produces explanations and retrieves supporting documents asynchronously.

+   - **Fast-path latency**: p99 end-to-end classification latency below 10 ms at the declared burst load;
+   - **Slow-path generation**: p95 time-to-first-token below 250 ms for asynchronous explanations, with no dependency from order routing on this service;
+   - **Throughput**: sustain 10,000 normalized text events per second during synthetic stress periods after upstream filtering;
+   - **Quality**: macro-F1 of at least 0.94 on a frozen, time-separated sentiment and relevance test set, with calibration error below the approved threshold;
+   - **Reliability**: at least 99.95% availability during defined market hours, with replayable inputs and no unreconciled event loss;
+   - **Compliance**: immutable lineage for source events, transformations, model and prompt versions, retrieved evidence, scores, generated explanations, and downstream use; and
+   - **Cost**: annualized run-rate at or below 70% of the defined cloud-only baseline, including development, deployment, hardware or cloud spend, power, cooling, engineering, monitoring, maintenance, scaling, and disaster recovery.
