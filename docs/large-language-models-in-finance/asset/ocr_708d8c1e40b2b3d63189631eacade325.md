A typical pipeline for macro events includes:

+   1. retrieving historical event documents and associated market reactions,
+   2. training models to map textual features to realized price/curve moves [Bholat et al., 2015],
+   3. using LLMs to process new events into the same feature space in real time,
+   4. combining textual signals with order-book and flow information [Menkveld, 2013].

# 7.1.4 Multi-agent research workstations

Research at asset managers and hedge funds involves:

+   • screening instruments based on fundamentals, valuation, and themes [Asness et al., 2013],
+   • reading and synthesizing large volumes of text,
+   • building and maintaining models of cash flows and risk,
+   • writing investment notes and recommendations.

LLM agents, as introduced in Chapter 5, can orchestrate this workflow under human supervision [Wang et al., 2024, Xi et al., 2025]. A typical research copilot may coordinate:

+   • a retriever agent (RAG over filings, transcripts, research) [Lewis et al., 2020],
+   • a summarizer agent (company snapshot, key metrics, recent events) [Zhang et al., 2020],
+   • a risk agent (governance, legal, macro risks, ESG) [Berg et al., 2022],
+   • a thesis agent (draft investment thesis with pros/cons).
