but it penalizes valid paraphrases and rewards superficially similar but potentially wrong text. BERTScore addresses the paraphrase problem by comparing contextual embeddings of predicted and reference tokens—two sentences with the same meaning receive a high score even if they share no words. Human evaluation remains the gold standard in financial summarization because it is the only method that can reliably detect factual errors such as an incorrectly stated revenue figure or an omitted guidance revision. In high-stakes financial settings, automated metrics should always be supplemented by at least a sample-level human audit before a summarization model is deployed in production.

With this foundation in place, we now move beyond supervised signals to settings where explicit labels are not always available, and alignment must be learned from human preferences.

# ## 3.7 Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

The fine-tuning methods discussed so far assume that the desired model behavior can be fully specified through labeled examples: a sentence paired with a sentiment label, a question paired with a correct answer, a transcript paired with a gold summary. For many financial applications this assumption holds. But a growing class of tasks—generating investment commentary, drafting client-facing explanations of portfolio decisions, writing nuanced risk narratives—resists clean labeling because quality depends on subtle, subjective attributes: Is the tone appropriately confident without being overconfident? Does the response include the right regulatory disclaimers? Is the reasoning transparent enough for a compliance officer to follow?

RLHF addresses this by replacing the fixed label with a learned reward signal derived from human judgments. Rather than telling the model exactly what to output, it tells the model which of several candidate outputs humans prefer, then uses reinforcement learning to optimize the policy toward higher-scoring responses. This makes RLHF the natural complement to supervised fine-tuning: SFT teaches the model *what* to do, while RLHF teaches it *how well* to do it according to human standards that resist precise formalization.

To understand how RLHF operates in practice, it is useful to first look at the overall pipeline.
