# ## 9.6 Challenges and future directions

Although RL offers useful tools for aligning financial LLMs with feedback and long-term objectives, applying it in finance remains difficult. The main challenges come from reward design, limited data, changing market conditions, explainability requirements, and the high cost of errors.

# ### 9.6.1 Key challenges in RL for financial LLMs

The application of RL to financial LLMs faces several unique challenges:

+   1. **Reward specification**: Financial objectives are difficult to reduce to a single reward function. A poorly designed reward may encourage reward hacking, excessive risk-taking, or responses that appear useful but are inaccurate or non-compliant. Research on preference-based RL and RLHF shows both the value of learned feedback and the importance of reward-model quality [Christiano et al., 2017, Ouyang et al., 2022].
