| Architecture | Best suited to | Main advantages | Main controls and limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Long-context prompting | A bounded, known set of documents that fits comfortably within the effective context budget | Preserves full-document order and avoids retrieval misses | Higher token cost and latency; irrelevant text can dilute evidence; permissions must be enforced before prompt construction |
| RAG | Large or frequently changing repositories and focused factual questions | Lower context cost; metadata filtering; source-level access control; easier corpus updates | Retrieval can miss decisive passages; requires evaluation of recall, reranking, chunking, and citation faithfulness |
| Document graph | Cross-references, amendments, entity relationships, temporal dependencies, and contradiction analysis | Makes document relationships explicit and supports structured consistency checks | More ingestion and maintenance effort; graph construction errors can propagate into synthesis |
| Hybrid | High-stakes analysis over a large corpus | Uses retrieval or graphs to select evidence, then supplies complete relevant sections or documents to a long-context model | Most robust but operationally complex; requires end-to-end testing, provenance, and cost controls |

# Table 8.1: When to use long context, RAG, a document graph, or a hybrid design

Long-context prompting is appropriate when the evidence set is small, stable, permission-compatible, and comfortably below the effective context limit. RAG is preferable when the corpus is large, dynamic, or access-controlled, or when queries are narrow and repeated. A document graph becomes valuable when the answer depends on amendment chains, entity resolution, period alignment, or cross-document consistency rather than simple passage retrieval. For many financial applications, the most defensible design is hybrid: retrieve and rerank candidate materials, expand to complete sections, preserve explicit relationships, and then synthesize within a controlled long-context request.
