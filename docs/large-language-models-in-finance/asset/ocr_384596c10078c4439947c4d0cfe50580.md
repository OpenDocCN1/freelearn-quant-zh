| Channel | Typical violation | Point-in-time control |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Artifact vintage | Dictionary, tokenizer, base model, task head, prompt, calibrator, or retrieval index uses post-date information. | Version and audit the complete artifact; require prefix-causal provenance and deployment before use. |
| 2 | Label realization | A model dated _t_ trains on an h-day outcome whose terminal price is unknown at _t_. | Train only on labels whose known date is strictly before the build vintage. |
| 3 | Transformation vintage | Full-sample standardization, winsorization, ranks, IDF, calibration, or feature selection. | Fit every transform on a lagged prefix and stamp it as part of the artifact. |
| 4 | Timestamp actionability | Revision or vendor stamps replace first availability; latency and order cutoffs are ignored. | First availability, venue cutoffs, latency, early-close calendars, explicit execution timing. |
| 5 | Corpus survivorship | Current constituents, tickers, revised text, or backfilled archives projected into the past. | Point-in-time entity maps, universes, corporate actions, delistings, as-first-available text. |
| 6 | Evaluation selection | Hyperparameters, prompts, horizons, or periods selected on the final statistic. | Pre-specification, nested walk-forward selection, search disclosure, untouched holdout. |

Table 13.4: Six channels of look-ahead in text signals.

Channels 1–4 are closable in code conditional on auditable inputs; channels 5–6 also require contracts and governance.

Channel 1 is broader than “model weights”: a tokenizer can reveal future vocabulary, a prompt can be selected after inspecting the test period, and a mutable API alias can silently change the model used to replay a backtest. Channel 3 is likewise broader than a _z_-score; every estimated transform has a vintage of its own.

# 13.5.3 What a clean pipeline actually guarantees

Five signal-level assumptions make the guarantee precise, with a sixth, ex-ante research design, governing evaluation selection: **(D1)** text and event metadata are as-first-available, with revisions versioned rather than substituted; **(D2)** prices, entity resolution, universes, and delistings are point-in-time; **(D3)** every data-dependent component of (13.3) has a documented prefix-causal build map, with supervised parts trained only on realized labels; **(D4)** build, release, deployment, inference, and routing complete before the relevant cutoff, with conservative latency in (13.4); and **(D5)** code, dependencies, model or API version, prompt, retrieval snapshot, and random state are pinned, so replay never queries mutable future state. Let \(\mathcal{H}_d^\text{PIT}\) be the information generated by all records available by _q<sub>d</sub>_, the pre-specified maps, and only those artifacts satisfying D3–D5, and call two complete histories _d_-equivalent when they agree on these admissible inputs through _q<sub>d</sub>_.
