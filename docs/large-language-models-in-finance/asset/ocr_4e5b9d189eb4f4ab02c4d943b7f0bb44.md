They can. The decisive difference is that transformers process sequences in parallel through self-attention and allow direct interaction between distant tokens, which improves computational efficiency and strengthens the modeling of long-range dependencies that are critical in financial applications [Vaswani et al., 2017].

The impact of transformers on financial AI stems from their ability to connect disparate financial concepts, entities, and temporal patterns within long documents and data streams. In a sequential model, information about an early covenant, risk disclosure, or macroeconomic event must propagate through many recurrent steps before it affects a later representation. In a transformer, the attention mechanism can directly relate that earlier token or passage to a later one, regardless of distance. This is especially valuable in finance, where a current valuation, risk assessment, or compliance conclusion may depend on information disclosed many pages or many reporting periods earlier.
