Consistency for an expected risk is a separate probabilistic statement: it requires a law of large numbers for the sequence of realized test losses under the target deployment regime. The embargo reduces near-boundary contamination, but it does not by itself remove revised filings, delayed labels, duplicate news, vendor backfills, or incorrect availability timestamps. Those channels require separate revision-chain, label-availability, deduplication, and vendor-timestamp controls. If a test-period artifact enters training or selection, the fitted pipeline becomes a function of a strictly larger sigma-algebra than $\mathcal{F}_{\tau_{\text{train}}}$; the evaluation then conditions on information that would not have been available at deployment time.
