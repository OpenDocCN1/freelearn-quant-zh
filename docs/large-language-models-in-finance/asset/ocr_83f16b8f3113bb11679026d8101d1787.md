# 7.2 Middle office: Risk management and surveillance

While front-office applications focus on market opportunities and trading decisions, middle-office applications focus on measuring, monitoring, and controlling risk. Middle-office functions translate trading and banking activities into risk profiles, limits, and regulatory reports. LLMs assist by structuring free-text information, generating scenarios, and prioritizing human attention.
