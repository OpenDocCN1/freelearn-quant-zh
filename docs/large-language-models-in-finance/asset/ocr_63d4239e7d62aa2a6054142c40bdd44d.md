For this document class, the extraction schema should explicitly represent:

+   * tranche seniority, balances, coupons, and legal final maturities;
+   * each waterfall step and the conditions under which it applies;
+   * coverage-test definitions, numerators, denominators, thresholds, and cure mechanics;
+   * reinvestment-period rules and asset-eligibility criteria;
+   * concentration limits, collateral-quality tests, and manager fees; and
+   * links between extracted clauses and any cash-flow or stress-testing model that consumes them.

A production workflow should test the resulting structured representation against known examples and reconcile extracted balances, percentages, and waterfall priorities before the data reach a risk engine. Once validated, the structured terms can support covenant monitoring, exception management, and portfolio-level analysis of contractual exposures [Chen et al., 2024b].

# 8.1.2 Automated compliance checks for SEC and FINRA materials

Public companies and regulated intermediaries operate under detailed filing, disclosure, communication, approval, and recordkeeping requirements. LLMs can help identify potentially missing disclosures, inconsistent narratives, unsupported claims, or risky language, but they should not issue final legal or compliance determinations. The appropriate output is a documented finding for qualified human review.

# A layered verification design

Compliance review is strongest when tasks are allocated to the component best suited to them:

+   * **Structural checks** verify required sections, signatures, dates, exhibits, and document versions using deterministic rules;
+   * **Quantitative checks** reconcile tables, totals, periods, percentages, and XBRL facts with ordinary code;
+   * **Semantic checks** use classifiers or LLMs to flag disclosure gaps, inconsistent statements, boilerplate risk factors, and unsupported claims;
+   * **Evidence checks** require every semantic finding to cite the relevant source passage and the policy or rule used by the checker; and
+   * **Human disposition** records whether a reviewer accepts, rejects, or remediates each finding.
