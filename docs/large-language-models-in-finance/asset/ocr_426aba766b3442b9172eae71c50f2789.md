| BloombergGPT | Finance-heavy pre-training of a decoder-only foundation model; financial NLP and general-language tasks. | Multi-task benchmark comparison reported by the original authors; not a claim of profitable deployment. | Primary study with substantive methodological detail. Main limits: proprietary training data, limited independent reproducibility, and benchmark contamination uncertainty. |
| FinBERT | Domain-adapted encoder for financial classification, especially sentiment. | Public or research datasets with supervised labels; results depend strongly on dataset and label definition. | Reproducible model family, but many downstream claims reuse small or randomly split benchmarks. Temporal and entity leakage remain material risks. |
| FinEAS | Sentence-BERT-derived encoder with a bounded regression head for continuous financial-news sentiment. | RavenPack-based experiments reported in the original study, including random-split results that are not equivalent to point-in-time evaluation. | Primary study; Chapter 13 reconstructs the leakage implication. Main risk: temporal interpolation and near-duplicate news. |
| FinGPT | Open systems framework using data pipelines, instruction tuning, and parameter-efficient adaptation. | A collection of task- and implementation-level demonstrations rather than one stable benchmark protocol. | Useful open engineering artifacts. Claims must be evaluated per dataset, checkpoint, prompt, adapter, and release; project breadth is not itself evidence of financial value. |
| FinMem | Memory-augmented single-agent trading design. | Backtests reported over selected asset sets and periods, using a particular memory and prompting configuration. | Primary research prototype. Main risks: short samples, data availability, transaction-cost assumptions, prompt selection, and version drift. |
| TradingAgents / FinCon / FinAgent | Role-based or debate-based multi-agent research and portfolio systems. | Simulation or backtest results under project-specific orchestration, tools, and market windows. | Early-stage primary studies. Agent count and debate quality do not substitute for point-in-time data, realistic frictions, ablations, or independent replication. |
| FinQA / TAT-QA style systems | Program-supervised numerical reasoning over financial reports and tables. | Exact-answer and program-execution metrics on curated question-answering datasets. | Strong benchmark evidence for bounded document reasoning. Main limits: dataset scope, annotation artifacts, and the gap between benchmark arithmetic and live financial workflows. |
