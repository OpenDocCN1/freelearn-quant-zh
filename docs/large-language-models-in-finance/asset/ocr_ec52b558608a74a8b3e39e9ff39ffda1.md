# Who this book is for

This book is intended for readers who are concerned with the future of financial intelligence and the responsible deployment of AI in financial systems.

For quantitative researchers and traders, the book provides methods to incorporate language and unstructured text into traditional models, time-series pipelines, and portfolio construction. For risk managers and compliance officers, it examines mechanisms for controlling, monitoring, and auditing AI-driven decisions under demanding regulatory and model risk frameworks. For technology leaders and architects, it offers blueprints for deployment and governance, including integration patterns, infrastructure choices, and observability. For academics, it presents a synthesis of natural language processing, reinforcement learning, and financial economics. For policymakers and regulators, it illustrates both the promise and the risk of embedding LLMs into the core of global financial systems.

The book assumes familiarity with basic machine learning, Python, financial markets, and core concepts in risk, compliance, or quantitative analysis. Readers do not need to be experts in every area, but they should be comfortable moving between mathematical notation, code examples, and financial workflows.

# What this book covers

Chapter 1, Introduction to Large Language Models, grounds the core concepts and situates LLMs within the evolution of financial AI.

Chapter 2, Foundations and System Design of Financial LLMs, develops the underlying Transformer architecture, tokenization schemes, pre-training, and prompt engineering required for real-world systems.

Chapter 3, Fine-Tuning LLMs for Finance, presents both full and parameter-efficient adaptation methods and their application to financial tasks.

Chapter 4, Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Financial Tasks, introduces the RAG paradigm, embedding models, vector databases, and evaluation methods for knowledge-intensive financial applications.

Chapter 5, Architectures and Applications of LLM Agents in Finance, explores agentic architectures, multi-agent systems, and tool integration for trading, risk, and operations.
