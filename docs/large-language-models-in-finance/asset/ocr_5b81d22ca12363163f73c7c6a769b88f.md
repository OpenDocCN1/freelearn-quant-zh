Notice that the risk review occurs after the draft memo has been produced, not merely at the beginning of the workflow. This is because compliance issues often arise from the specific wording of conclusions, assumptions, and recommended actions. The same pattern can be extended with earlier checks as well, such as restricted-list screening before the research step.
