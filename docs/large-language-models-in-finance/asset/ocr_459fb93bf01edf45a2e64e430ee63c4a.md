# Advanced Topics: Reasoning, Multimodality, and Time Series LLMs in Finance

This chapter explores frontier areas where Large Language Models (LLMs) extend beyond traditional applications in finance. It begins with advanced reasoning techniques such as Chain-of-Thought and Tree of Thoughts, examining how they enhance the ability of LLMs to tackle complex financial problems. The discussion then turns to multimodality, highlighting how models can integrate textual, numerical, and visual data streams to produce richer market insights and document intelligence. Finally, the chapter analyzes recent innovations in time-series LLMs for forecasting, including tokenization methods, architectural advances, empirical benchmarks, deployment strategies, and the methodological pitfalls that can invalidate financial backtests. Together, these sections provide a forward-looking but disciplined view of how reasoning, multimodal processing, and temporal modeling are shaping the next generation of financial AI systems.

In this chapter, we cover the following topics:

+   - Advanced reasoning in financial LLMs
+   - Multimodality in financial analysis
+   - Time-series LLMs in finance
+   - Pitfalls of LLMs in financial forecasting and backtesting
+   - Integration and future directions
+   - Open research questions and recommended resources
