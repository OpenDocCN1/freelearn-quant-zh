+   5. **Causal multimodal forecasting:** Can textual events, images, audio, and market paths be combined in models that separate predictive association from intervention-relevant mechanisms?

+   6. **Contamination-resistant benchmarks:** How can time series foundation models and financial LLMs be evaluated when pretraining corpora, public filings, benchmark periods, and model selection decisions may overlap with the nominal test window?

+   7. **Adaptive tokenization without semantic drift:** How can learned vocabularies evolve with market regimes while preserving comparability, interpretability, and stable downstream controls?

+   8. **Institutional and systemic effects:** What happens when many institutions deploy similar reasoning and forecasting systems, creating correlated trades, shared failure modes, or feedback between model outputs and the data on which future models are trained?

# 13.7.2 Recommended reading and practical resources

Readers seeking deeper coverage can use the following grouped pathway:

+   • **Reasoning and search:** Chain-of-Thought, Tree of Thoughts, self-refinement, test-time compute, and MCTS provide the conceptual sequence from linear decomposition to budgeted search and iterative critique [Wei et al., 2022b, Yao et al., 2023a, Madaan et al., 2023, Snell et al., 2024, Kocsis and Szepesvári, 2006, Browne et al., 2012].

+   • **Multimodal representation and document intelligence:** CLIP and Vision Transformers establish shared representation and visual-token foundations, while HuBERT, LayoutLMv3, and Donut extend the discussion to audio and document structure [Radford et al., 2021, Dosovitskiy et al., 2021, Hsu et al., 2021, Huang et al., 2022, Kim et al., 2022].

+   • **Time series transformers and foundation models:** PatchTST, Chronos, iTransformer, Time-LLM, and TimeGPT illustrate complementary approaches to patching, quantization, cross-variate attention, language-guided reprogramming, and large-scale temporal pretraining [Nie et al., 2023, Ansari et al., 2024, Liu et al., 2024d, Jin et al., 2024, Garza and Mergenhaler-Canseco, 2024].

+   • **Causal foundations:** Structural causal models and causal discovery provide the formal distinction between association, intervention, and counterfactual reasoning [Pearl, 2009, Peters et al., 2017].

+   • **Governance and supervision:** The EU AI Act, the scope-limited U.S. interagency model-risk guidance, the NIST AI RMF and Generative AI Profile, and the IOSCO supervisory toolkit offer complementary legal, organizational, and supervisory perspectives. Read SR 26-2 together with its explicit exclusion of generative and agentic AI rather than treating it as a general LLM standard [European Parliament and Council of the European Union, 2024, Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System et al., 2026, National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2023, 2024, International Organization of Securities Commissions, 2026].
