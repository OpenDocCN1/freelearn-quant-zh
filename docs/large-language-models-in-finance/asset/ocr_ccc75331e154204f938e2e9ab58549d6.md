The main lesson is methodological. Time-series LLMs may become valuable components of financial forecasting systems, but their evaluation must be stricter than ordinary machine-learning evaluation, not looser. The more capable the model, the larger the surface area for hidden leakage. Robustness comes from treating the entire LLM system—base model, prompt, tools, retrieval, memory, data, and researcher workflow—as part of the backtest.
