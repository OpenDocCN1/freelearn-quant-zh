plus the exact definitions and adjustment sources. The LLM helps interpret and map language; it must not invent an add-back or silently choose among competing definitions.

**Evaluation.** Test clause extraction, definition linking, amendment precedence, numerical agreement with expert calculations, breach classification, and reviewer correction. Report performance separately for standard and highly negotiated documents. A high average extraction score is insufficient if the model misses rare but material exceptions.

**Stop conditions.** Escalate when amendments conflict, a defined term cannot be resolved, source figures are unaudited or unavailable, permitted adjustments require judgment, or the calculated headroom is within a documented uncertainty band.

# 7.4.4 Case 4: Governed portfolio-research workstation

**Decision supported.** A portfolio manager asks for an evidence-linked comparison of several securities, scenario effects on the existing portfolio, and a draft investment memo. The system may support a proposal but does not bypass the order-management, mandate, pre-trade, or approval controls.

**Pipeline architecture.** A supervisor coordinates specialist components for filings, news, quantitative analysis, portfolio risk, and compliance. Each specialist receives the minimum context required for its task and returns a typed object. The supervisor cannot execute trades. A separate policy service checks restricted lists, client mandates, concentration, liquidity, turnover, and approval requirements.
