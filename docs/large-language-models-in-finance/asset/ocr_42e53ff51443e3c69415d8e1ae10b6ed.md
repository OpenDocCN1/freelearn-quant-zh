weighting in (12.14) is the entire content of the method, and an implementation that drops it optimizes expected P&L with extra variance and a risk-management label.

Reported backtests on small equity portfolios indicate that FinCon can achieve higher cumulative return and Sharpe ratio than Markowitz and deep RL baselines on the tested portfolios, while maintaining equal or lower drawdowns.

# FinAgent: Multimodal tool-augmented agent

FinAgent integrates multiple modalities, including:

+   - A price encoder \(\phi_{price}\) mapping historical price sequences to latent features;
+   - A news encoder \(\phi_{news}\) (often an LLM or FinBERT) producing text embeddings;
+   - A chart or image encoder \(\phi_{chart}\) capturing technical patterns.

A simple fusion architecture is:

```
z_{price} = \phi_{price}(p_{t-L:t}), \qquad (12.15)
z_{news} = \phi_{news}(\mathcal{N}_t), \qquad (12.16)
z_{chart} = \phi_{chart}(C_t), \qquad (12.17)
z_{fusion} = \psi(z_{price}, z_{news}, z_{chart}), \qquad (12.18)
```

where \(\psi\) is a learned fusion network (e.g. concatenation followed by MLPs and attention). The policy is then formulated:

```
a_t \sim \pi_\theta(a \mid z_{fusion}, tools_t),
```

with tool outputs (indicators, risk calculations) as additional inputs. Training is typically done via RL or imitation learning from expert demonstrations.

In reported experiments on selected equities and crypto-assets, FinAgent achieves higher annualized returns and Sharpe ratios than baseline models for the tested periods, with drawdowns that remain within risk-tolerant boundaries. Gains are largest where text and chart patterns interact strongly with price dynamics.

# 12.2.4 Backtesting caveats

Across FinMem, TradingAgents, FinCon, FinAgent and similar systems, a set of methodological caveats recur:

+   - **Limited scope:** Many studies focus on a handful of assets over one or two market regimes.
+   - **Simplified costs:** Transaction costs and market impact are often modeled crudely or omitted.
+   - **Short horizons:** Some reported Sharpe ratios are computed over short windows, making them sensitive to a few favourable periods.
