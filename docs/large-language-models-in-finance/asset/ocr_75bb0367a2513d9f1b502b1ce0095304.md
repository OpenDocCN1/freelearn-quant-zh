# 7.3.3 KYC and AML workflows

Know your customer (KYC) and anti-money laundering (AML) processes rely on:

+   - identification documents,
+   - corporate registries and beneficial ownership data,
+   - address verification,
+   - transaction-monitoring systems, and
+   - adverse-media and sanctions screening [Weber et al., 2018].

LLMs support these processes by:

+   - parsing and normalizing documents into structured forms,
+   - synthesizing customer profiles (including complex corporate structures),
+   - summarizing adverse-media hits in a standardized way, and
+   - drafting suspicious activity reports (SARs) with clear, consistent language.

Importantly, LLMs operate under the following strict constraints:

+   - they do not decide onboarding or de-risking outcomes; they support analysts;
+   - they do not replace sanctions-screening engines or rule-based AML systems; they explain and prioritize alerts;
+   - all outputs are subject to human review and regulatory requirements [European Union, 2015].
