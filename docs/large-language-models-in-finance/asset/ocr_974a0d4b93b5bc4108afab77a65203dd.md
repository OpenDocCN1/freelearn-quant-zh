+   1. collect raw documents (news, filings, transcripts),
+   2. clean, deduplicate, and map records to point-in-time issuer and security identifiers,
+   3. encode each document into a vector or structured summary [Reimers and Gurevych, 2019, Gao et al., 2021],
+   4. aggregate per instrument and time bucket, such as a day, hour, or event window, into z_t.

Aggregation may be as simple as averaging embeddings or as sophisticated as attention-based pooling weighted by recency or source reliability [Yang et al., 2016, Lin et al., 2017].
